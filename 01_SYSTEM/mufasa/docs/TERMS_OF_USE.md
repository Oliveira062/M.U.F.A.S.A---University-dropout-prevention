# MUFASA Terms of Use

## 1. What MUFASA is

MUFASA is an internal system of [university name] for early warning and student retention support. It organizes academic and context indicators, calculates a risk level explained by factors and suggests actions to the pedagogical team.

MUFASA is **not** a student assessment system, it does **not** predict anyone's future and it does **not** make decisions. Every action about a student depends on an identified person, who confirms and records what they did (SRS, RN05; LGPD, art. 20).

## 2. Who can use it

Only people with an active link to the university and with an assigned profile: student, teacher, coordination, support center, finance office, board, technical administration and data protection officer. Each profile sees only what its role requires.

## 3. Allowed use

MUFASA serves exclusively to identify students who may need support and to organize that support.

## 4. Forbidden use

It is forbidden to use any information or result from MUFASA for:

- selection, merit assessment or grading;
- granting or denying a scholarship, aid or any benefit;
- dismissal, disciplinary sanction or public ranking of students, teachers or classes;
- any commercial purpose;
- exposing a student to classmates or third parties.

It is also forbidden to:

- share a login, password or token, or use another person's account;
- try to access data outside your profile, for example by changing the student ID in the address;
- copy, export or photograph data outside the system without formal authorization;
- automate queries, test security flaws without written authorization from the university or get around technical limits.

Improper access attempts are blocked and recorded.

## 5. Responsibilities of users

- Keep the password secret and change it if you suspect a leak.
- Sign out on shared computers.
- Treat everything you see in MUFASA as confidential information, even after leaving the role.
- Tell the data protection officer, at [email], about any suspected incident.

## 6. About the risk level

The risk level is a statistical indication, calculated with public rules described in the SRS (Annex A). It can be wrong, especially when data is missing. That is why:

- every result shows the factors that produced it and the completeness of the data;
- below 40% of available data, the system does not calculate and shows "Undetermined";
- teachers and coordination can mark a classification as wrong;
- the student can ask for a review of any result.

## 7. Availability

The university makes an effort to keep MUFASA available, but does not guarantee uninterrupted operation. Urgent services do not depend on the system and follow the institutional channels.

## 8. Privacy

The processing of personal data follows the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and the [Cookie Policy](COOKIE_POLICY.md).

## 9. Non-compliance

Use that does not follow these terms may lead to suspension of access and to an inquiry into responsibility according to the university's bylaws and the applicable law, including the LGPD.

## 10. Changes

Changes to these terms get a new version and date and are presented on the next first access, with a request for new acceptance.

## 11. Jurisdiction

The courts of the district of [city/state] are chosen, unless the law provides otherwise.
